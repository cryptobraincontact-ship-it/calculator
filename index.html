    Loading Instructions

      Vehicles (internal dimensions)

      Default dimensions are approximate and vary by body builder. Enter measured internal dimensions for accurate results (7 ft 6 in = 7.5 ft). Settings are saved in this browser.

      Clearances (cm)

      Keep at least 10 cm (4 in) above the load for air circulation. Do not load above the red load line.

      Floor layouts use guillotine-cut optimisation. Mixed loads are placed in sections from cabin to door. Actual results may vary with pallets, dunnage and carton bulge.
